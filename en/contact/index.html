FAQ / Q&A

Questions about addiction counselling

How much is a consultation?

A 60-minute individual consultation costs AMD 25,000.

How can I make an appointment?

Call +374 98 098 774 or message via WhatsApp. Appointments are arranged in advance.

Is the consultation confidential?

Respect for privacy is a central part of the process. The scope and limits of confidentiality are explained during consultation.

When does gambling become an addiction?

Warning signs include losing control, hiding financial losses and continuing to gamble despite harm. A professional consultation can help assess the situation.

Is one session enough?

It depends on your situation. An initial consultation helps identify what further support, if any, may be useful.

Do you prescribe medication?

This website describes psychological consultations. Prescriptions and medical detoxification require appropriately licensed physicians.
